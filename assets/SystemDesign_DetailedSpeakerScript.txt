SLIDE 3: What is System Design? (Enhanced)

System design is the art and science of building large-scale distributed systems that can handle real-world demands. It's not just about making things work - it's about making them work at scale, reliably, and efficiently. When we talk about performance, we're discussing concrete metrics: Can your API respond in under 100 milliseconds? Can your system handle 10,000 requests per second? Scalability means growing from your first thousand users to a billion without rewriting everything. Reliability is measured in 'nines' - 99.99% uptime means only 52 minutes of downtime per year. That's the standard for critical systems. The real-world impact is staggering: Netflix serves 200 million users streaming 4K video simultaneously. A poor architectural decision could cost millions in infrastructure or lost revenue. System design is where computer science meets business reality - every decision has technical and financial implications.
